Research

I want robots that imagine and reason about the consequences of their actions before they act. My current work builds generative world models that predict physical dynamics directly in 3D and uses them to plan contact-rich manipulation. I am also interested in faster planning and inference with diffusion and flow models, real-world policy adaptation with reinforcement learning, and runtime safety for robots working around people. I have also worked on learning from large offline demonstration datasets and on generating legible robot motion that leads to better human robot collaboration.
